Our Story

Founded in 2024, Logan's Coffee Co. started with one goal: to create a space where Pretoria can come together over great coffee, for students and workers in Pretoria CBD. Our passion for coffee and community drives everything we do, from sourcing the finest beans to serving the perfect cup.

We believe in quality, sustainability, and the power of a good cup of coffee to bring people together. Our team is dedicated to providing exceptional experience for every customer, whether you're here for a quick espresso or a leisurely afternoon with friends.

We source only single-origin, ethically sourced beans. Small-batch roasted daily. Every latte is handcrafted by our expert baristas. We never compromise on taste.

Students, workers, families - everyone is welcome. Free WiFi, cozy corners, workspace tables. We host Volunteer Barista Days and Community Brews every month.

We aim to minimize our environmental impact. Compostable cups, burlap sack recycling, local sourcing. Our burlap tag says "Small Batch - Roasted Daily - Est. 2018" because we care.

Honey Sage Cold Brew, Lavender Latte, Pink Strawberry Matcha - we brew seasonal specials. Our baristas experiment weekly to delight you.

Our Mission

To serve quality coffee and create a welcoming environment for our community.
To fuel the city with great coffee, good vibes, and exceptional service.
